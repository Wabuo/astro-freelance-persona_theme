I'm **Lancy**, the official mascot of this theme.

## Who am I?

I am the automated spirit that lives in your `node_modules`. I was born from a stray `npm install` and raised by a strict linter.
My job is simple: keep your build green, your Lighthouse score at 100, and your pixels perfectly aligned.

## My Mission

- **Sweeping away legacy code:** I hate unused CSS.
- **Optimising assets:** I eat kilobytes for breakfast.
- **Enforcing type safety:** `any` is not in my vocabulary.

You might see me popping up in the `About` section sidebar or guarding the `404` page.
I'm here to serve.

## Showcasing MathJax Capabilities

To prove that my mathematical coprocessor is fully operational, I've compiled some advanced test equations using our bundled MathJax plugins:

### 1. Quantum Physics ( `physics` & `color` )
The time-dependent Schrödinger equation, styled dynamically using our CSS variable palette to adapt cleanly between light and dark modes:

```math
i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\ket{\psi(t)} = \textcolor{var(--accent-color)}{\hat{H}}\ket{\psi(t)}
```

Using the `physics` extension, we can write complex differentials and Dirac brackets cleanly:

```math
\braket{\psi | \hat{H} | \psi} = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \psi^*(x) \left( -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2} + V(x) \right) \psi(x) \, \dd{x}
```

### 2. Chemistry ( `mhchem` & `cancel` )
Here is a complex chemical redox reaction showing intermediate cancellations:

```chem
2\textcolor{red}{I^{-}} + \textcolor{blue}{S2O8^{2-}} -> \textcolor{red}{I2} + \textcolor{blue}{\cancel{S2O8^{2-}}} + 2\textcolor{gray}{e^{-}}
```

And a standard equilibrium synthesis reaction showing states of matter — with the atoms tinted so you can follow each one through the reaction (carbon red, hydrogen blue):

```chem
\textcolor{red}{C}O2(g) + \textcolor{blue}{H2}O(l) <=> \textcolor{blue}{H2}\textcolor{red}{C}O3(aq)
```

### 3. Delimiters & Extensible Arrows ( `mathtools` & `cancel` )
Cancelling terms in algebraic ratios and utilising extensible mapping arrows:

```math
\frac{\textcolor{red}{a(x + y)}}{\cancel{a}} \xrightarrow[\textcolor{gray}{\text{simplify}}]{\textcolor{blue}{\text{algebra}}} \textcolor{red}{x + y}
```

MathJax-set colours also work as visual grouping — here the distributed factor keeps its color as it escapes the brackets, and the matching terms are easy to track:

```math
\textcolor{red}{a}(x + y) = \textcolor{red}{a}x + \textcolor{red}{a}y \qquad (x + y)\textcolor{blue}{b} = x\textcolor{blue}{b} + y\textcolor{blue}{b}
```
